Why threeQ

A meeting exists to answer three questions

threeQ stands for three questions. It checks the conversation against the goals you set before the meeting and shows where there’s an answer and where there isn’t.

  1. What was decided
  2. Who’s on it
  3. By when
  4. What’s still open

Every goal gets a status: decided, pushed back, never got to it. Not “we talked about the mockups” but “onboarding yes, report no.”

How it works

All it takes: two messages

  1. 1

    Before: your goals

    One message, in your own words. That’s what the meeting gets scored against.

  2. 2

    After: the transcript

    From Google Meet, Zoom, Teams or Wispr, as an audio file or plain text. threeQ never joins the call.

  3. 3

    Two minutes later: the answer

    What, who, by when. Only you see it. Share it if you want to.
